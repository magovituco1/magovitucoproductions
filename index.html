01WHO WE ARE

MAGIC,
BUILT FOR THE SEA.

We are an international company specializing in premium magical entertainment for cruise ships and ferries.

Founded by magician and producer Mago Vituco, the company was born from over 15 years of hands-on experience at sea, creating shows that perform night after night in any part of the world.

We have collaborated with Costa Cruises, Costa Asia, MSC Cruises, Baleària, Saga Cruises, and Astro Ocean Cruises, among other international operators.

We don’t sell tricks.
We create unforgettable experiences.
