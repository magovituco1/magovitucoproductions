01WHO WE ARE

MAGIC,
BUILT FOR THE SEA.

Mago Vituco Productions creates professional magic shows and live entertainment for cruise ships, ferries and international hospitality audiences. Our formats include theater illusions, guest entertainers, close-up magic and participatory activities.

Founded by magician and producer Mago Vituco, our work draws on more than 15 years of entertainment experience at sea — from resident productions to guest performances designed around the rhythm of a voyage.

Our experience includes collaborations with Costa Cruises, Costa Asia, MSC Cruises, Baleària, Saga Cruises and Astro Ocean Cruises, among other international operators.

We don’t sell tricks.
We create unforgettable experiences.
